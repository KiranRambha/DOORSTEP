Doorstep

What's it like to live here?

Enter a postcode or town in England or Wales. Doorstep pulls together nearby school ratings, the closest stations and the commute into London, recent sale prices for detached and semi-detached houses, and local crime. Everything comes from free government open data.

  • Schools: Ofsted inspection results
  • Stations and journeys: TfL Journey Planner (covers National Rail)
  • Prices: HM Land Registry Price Paid Data
  • Crime: data.police.uk
  • Flood risk: Environment Agency (England)
